On your phone alone the app does everything except friends. An online account, for friends and for sync between phones, is free and anyone can make one. The Android app is an APK from our GitHub releases, not from Google Play yet. In English, German, French, Spanish, Polish, Czech, Slovak, Hungarian, Russian and Ukrainian.

What opt-in means

The shaming is a joke about the genre. The part that is not a joke is that you switch it on yourself, one practice at a time.

  • Out of the box the app is yours alone. No account, no sign-in. Nothing leaves the phone.
  • With friends, every streak still starts private. You switch a streak on per practice. Until you do, a friend sees nothing about that practice.
  • Counts, rounds, totals and targets are never shared. Not with friends, and not readable by the server. A streak is a number of days.
  • Done-today notes are switched on per friend. You hear that a friend has practised only if you asked to, for that friend. There is no member directory, no feed and no public profile, so there is no public to be shamed in front of.

Friends see streaks, if you let them. Nobody sees counts.

A streak is the number of days in a row you practised. It is private until you publish it, and you publish it practice by practice, to the friends you have accepted. Counts, rounds, totals and targets stay on your devices, sealed.

A session counts for the day it started, and the streak holds until midnight after the next day, in whichever time zone gives you more time. Travel does not break it.

Notes and nudges between friends

For each friend you choose whether you want a note when they finish a practice. When a friend has not practised yet, you can poke them, once a day. That is the whole social feature, and it is deliberately small. There is no member directory, no feed and no public profile.

Accounts and friendship codes

Anyone can install the app and use it on their phone, with no account, and anyone can make an online account. It takes a passkey and nothing else: a name, an e-mail address and your gender are all optional, and an account made without a name gets a random one, such as Calm Otter, until you change it. A friendship code is how friends find each other: a member shares it as a link or a QR code, it works for seven days, and entering it makes you and the sender friends, whether your account is new or not. Membership in a Buddhist practice app says something about your beliefs, so nobody can look up who is in it.

Got a friendship link but no app? This page shows the code to paste into the app once it is installed.

A new phone

Sign in with the same passkey on a new phone and your counts come back, still sealed until they reach it. Where your password manager cannot unlock them that way, the recovery code you kept does.

Correcting a count

Miscounted, or brought a total over from paper? Type the count as it should be in the practice's settings. The change is kept in History as a correction. It moves the total and the round, never a day's count or a streak.

Works offline

Every count is written to your phone first and synced later. A retreat with no signal loses nothing. You can also use the app on one phone only, with no account.

Encryption, in plain words

Sealed on your phone

Sessions, counts, rounds, targets and notes are encrypted before they leave the device. The keys never leave your devices.

The server never sees a count

It stores sealed blobs it cannot open. It checks sizes and signatures and nothing else.

What it does see

Your account, your display name, your friends, whose friendship code made each friendship, and the streaks you chose to publish. It also sees when each session was logged and synced, by your phone's clock and by its own, and how large each sealed blob is.

No tracking

No analytics, no trackers, no third-party fonts or scripts. This site makes no requests to anyone else.
